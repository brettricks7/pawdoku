Privacy Policy — Poodoku

Effective date: August 20, 2026

The short version

Poodoku is a single-player puzzle game that runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress (highest unlocked level) is saved in local app storage on your phone. It never leaves the device.
  • Nothing else. No name, email, location, contacts, photos, or identifiers are read or sent anywhere.

Internet

The Play Store build does not request network access. The game does not call any server of ours.

Data retention

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Uninstalling Poodoku removes local progress from your device.

Children

Poodoku is a general-audience logic puzzle with light toilet humor. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
